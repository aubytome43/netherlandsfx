THE ACADEMY

An academy for people who want to understand the market

NetherlandsFX teaches forex trading as a discipline: how currencies are priced, how charts are read, how news moves price, and how risk is controlled before a position is opened. Lessons are delivered in person and online, supported by written notes, quizzes and a student portal — without signals — we teach the process, and you practise it on a demo account first.

WHY LEARN WITH US

A teaching method built
for real market conditions

NetherlandsFX was created for traders who want to understand what they are doing — not to follow signals. Our approach is patient, analytical and rooted in risk control.

Risk management first

Every course is built around protecting capital: position sizing, stop placement and drawdown control before profit targets.

Charts, not opinions

You learn to read market structure, candlesticks, support and resistance on real chart examples rather than memorising signals.

Structured curriculum

Modules and lessons build in a deliberate order, so each idea rests on the one before it instead of jumping between topics.

Small-group teaching

Classes are capped so there is room for questions, chart reviews and individual feedback on your trading journal.

Practice between classes

Assignments, quizzes and demo-account exercises turn each lesson into a habit you can repeat on your own.

Honest about the risk

We never sell guaranteed returns. You will hear clearly that most retail traders struggle, and why discipline matters.
